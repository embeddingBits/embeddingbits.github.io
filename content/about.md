## About Me

Hi, I'm <span style="color: #d79921">Amiitesh</span>.

I work with Linux and systems programming. I write C/C++, Golang, Zig cause I hate high level abstractions. I work with low-level systems like embedded systems, operating systems, networks, and I like system design. Apart from tech I like reading, writing blogs and music. I write blogs about things that I find interesting and ideas I'm experimenting with. I mainly post about tech, music, photography and on the books I read.

I like my software *libre* so I'm a **big advocate** of FOSS and I write/contribute to Open Source Software. You can check out some [software](https://codeberg.org/embeddingbits/) that I wrote and [rice](https://codeberg.org/embeddingbits/dotfiles).
