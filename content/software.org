* Favourite

- *Operating System:* I use [[https://voidlinux.org][Void Linux]].
- *Text/Code Editor:* I use [[https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/][Emacs]] and [[https://neovim.io/][Neovim]].
- *Window Manager:* I use [[https://isaacfreund.com/software/river/][River]].
- *Terminal and Shell:* I use [[https://codeberg.org/dnkl/foot][Foot]] combined with [[https://fishshell.com/][fish]] shell.
- *Dotfiles:* I use my own [[https://codeberg.org/embeddingbits/nixos-dotfiles][dotfiles]].
-----

* Utils

- *File Manager:* I use [[https://yazi-rs.github.io/][Yazi]] as my main and only file manager.
- *Web Browser:* I use [[https://www.firefox.com/][Firefox]]
- *PDF Viewer:* I use [[https://pwmt.org/projects/zathura/][Zathura]] and [[https://github.com/vedang/pdf-tools][pdf-tools]] in Emacs.
- *Misc:* For most of the other stuff like Word, Excel, etc., I use [[https://www.libreoffice.org/][LibreOffice]].

-----

* College

- *Reports and Documents:* I use [[https://typst.app/][Typst]] and I write in Org Mode and export it to [[https://www.latex-project.org/][LaTeX]].
- *Taking Notes:* I manage my college and personal notes with [[https://orgmode.org/][Org Mode]].
