## Neorg

I often maintain a lot of digital notes. I take notes on paper when in college and then convert them to digital notes once I reach home.

I follow a Zettelkasten way of taking notes and I have been using Obsidian to take notes. I came across Neorg which is a Neovim plugin to bring org mode in neovim.
