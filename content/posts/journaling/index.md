## Introduction
Hello!
This is my first non technical post that I'm making here. I wanted to write blogs to just sorta dump ideas or other stuff floating around my head. But I never really got the time to sit down and write a blog. Most of the times, its cause of college or I'm too lazy to get it done :/

At one point in college, there were so many stuff that I wanted to do but I couldnt keep track of it due to the amount of them being dumped like college project and assignment deadlines, college online test dates, personal learning goals, some of my personal projects that lie around incomplete and all of these added up with academics. It was just too much to keep track of that I missed a lot of the online tests that were kept in college and I ended up procrastinating a lot and lost some of my daily habits like reading books, fitness.

Few days back, I was gifted a field note and some bunch of sticky notes and I got the natural idea of maintaining a note for habit tracking and todo list, though digitalised versions do exist, my sole purpose was to avoid using my phone and not end in a endless state of doomscrolling.
